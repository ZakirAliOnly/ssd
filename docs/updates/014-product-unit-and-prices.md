# Update 014 - Product unit and adjacent selling prices

Product packaging offers Per piece, Per kit and Per vial in the Unit dropdown, retaining existing custom unit values. Both Add Product modals place editable piece and packet selling prices beside each other. Packet price continues to autofill from piece price times pieces per packet until manually overridden; Use calculated price restores autofill. Selected units use the existing base_unit field and invoice unit labels. No migration required.
