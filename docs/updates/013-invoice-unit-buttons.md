# Update 013 - Invoice selling unit buttons

Party and walk-in invoices show Piece and Packet buttons under Sell in. The selected unit is highlighted and exposed with aria-pressed. Selecting another unit reuses the existing price, amount and stock conversion handlers; selecting the active unit preserves any manually edited invoice rate. Packet selection shows the packet size.

Existing box products and saved units remain supported. The supplier purchase selector retains its dropdown. No migration required.
